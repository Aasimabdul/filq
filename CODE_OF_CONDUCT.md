# Code of Conduct

We want Fliq to be a welcoming, respectful open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share reproducible steps and relevant sanitized logs when reporting bugs.
- Respect privacy; do not post another person's files, credentials, or personal information.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes that increase security risk, memory use, complexity, or maintenance burden.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. The maintainer will review reports and take action considered appropriate, including editing or removing content or restricting participation. Please include links and context, and avoid reposting sensitive details publicly.
